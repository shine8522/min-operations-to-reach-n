minOperation(n): ek ek line ka safar

0 se n tak minimum steps (double ya +1). Hum n se 0 tak ulta chalte hain.

n-
count-
odd → n − 1
even → n ÷ 2
n ka safar (ulta raasta)

Interview cheat-sheet

  • Idea (30 sec): forward tree ki jagah n se peeche. Odd pe −1 (double se odd nahi banta), even pe ÷2 (do +1 ki jagah ek double).
  • Time O(log n), space O(1): odd ke baad n even ho jaata hai aur even pe aadha, toh bahut kam iterations.
  • Shortcut: floor(log2 n) + popcount(n). n=7: 2+3 = 5. n=8: 3+1 = 4. n=15: 3+4 = 7.
  • Edge cases: n=0 → 0 (loop chalta hi nahi). n=1 → 1 (odd, 1 → 0).
  • Even pe −1 kyun nahi? 6 pe −1,−1 → 4 (2 steps), ÷2 → 3 (1 step). Half ek step mein zyada neeche le jaata hai.
  • Bolna kya hai: "Last operation +1 thi ya double? Odd hai toh +1. Even hai toh double lena better hai. Isliye reverse mein greedy chalta hai."
